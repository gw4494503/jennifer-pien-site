The Physician

Jennifer Pien
MD

I am a board-certified psychiatrist and Clinical Associate Professor in the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at Stanford University. My clinical work centers on physician well-being through Stanford's WellConnect program, and I am on the Advisory Board for Bellevue Literary Press and the Oxford Review of Books x Stanford.

My training began at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine, where I earned my MD and I completed my psychiatry residency at Stanford.

Alongside my clinical practice, I have dedicated much of my career to the intersection of medicine and the literary arts. I am the director of the Pegasus Physician Writers, a creative writing and publication program within Stanford's Medical Humanities and the Arts and founded The Pegasus Review. I am a co-founder and literary agent at Hesperides Literary, and bring years of editorial and advisory experience to writers navigating the path toward publication.

Pegasus Physician Writers

A creative writing and publication program within Stanford's Medical Humanities and the Arts. Modeled on the Iowa Writers' Workshop, Pegasus brings together physicians and physicians-in-training for monthly workshops to develop and share their creative writing.

Home of the Irvin D. Yalom Literary Awards, the Marginalized Voices Workshop Series, and collaborations with Bellevue Literary Press and the Oxford Review of Books.

Visit pegasusphysicians.org →

02 — Literary Agency

Hesperides Literary

Co-founded with Connor Smith, Hesperides Literary represents writers working at the crossroads of medicine, narrative nonfiction, literary fiction, and the human experience. Jennifer brings deep editorial expertise and publishing relationships to every client.
